Ministries

At CBC, the Lord has called us to provide meaningful opportunities for individuals of all ages to grow in their faith, build lasting relationships, and find biblical guidance for every stage of life. From children to adults, the various ministries are dedicated to nurturing a love for God, a heart for serving others, and a passion for spreading the good news of Salvation (Ephesians 4:15; Deuteronomy 6:6-7; Titus 2:2-7; Matthew 28:19-20; 1 Peter 4:10).
